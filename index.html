Sterrify Daily use

Problem: Playing along meant switching between Spotify and Songsterr while holding an instrument.

What I built: An Electron app that syncs Songsterr to Spotify and switches instrument and tuning automatically. I then rebuilt it as Sterrify Tabs Private repo, a Spicetify app that renders the tab inside Spotify with alphaTab. The old app polled the Web API, which caused 465 ms of its 567 ms median sync lag. Running inside Spotify removes that delay.

Proof: I use it daily. Sterrify Tabs has an automated test suite.

Repo

Grapevine Built with a friend No longer in use

Why: A creative and technical experiment. We built our own social network, I talked our friend group into using it, and then we added games and an in-app economy to reward playing.

What we built: A Hebrew, right-to-left mobile app with posts, comments, custom Golden Grape and Raisin reactions, and push notifications.

Games: Two replaced party games we used to play with pen and paper or had to work hard to invent on the spot: Land City, a 14-category multi-page form with shared state, and a rule game that builds a fresh rule each round from a recursive grammar. Eshkolosseum is a nightly D&D-style auto-battle between our characters, run by a scheduled Node.js server, where players bet coins on the fighters. Eli Clicker is an idle game that cashes out into coins.

Economy: Players earn coins by winning bets and playing, and spend them in a store of 16 cosmetic packs that restyle their posts.

  • Expo
  • Appwrite
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • TypeScript

Friendmeter Daily use Private repo
